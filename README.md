# Heroes' Vow: Three Kingdoms — Officer Generator

An unofficial, fan-made character generator for **Heroes' Vow: Three Kingdoms**, designed to help players populate their games with unique, historically plausible fictional officers.

Instead of relying on completely arbitrary stat rolls, the generator creates coherent characters whose attributes, proficiencies, personalities, strategies, equipment, and backgrounds complement one another.

## Features

- **Randomized Officer Creation:** Generate original male and female officers with Chinese names, courtesy names, attributes, talents, and biographies.
- **Historically Inspired Backgrounds:** Create independent fictional officers or fictional relatives of famous Three Kingdoms figures, with plausible lifespans and historical connections.
- **Attribute-Based Proficiencies:** Stronger officers generally possess more developed skills, with dedicated primary weapon specializations.
- **Intelligent Strategy and Epithet Selection:** Strategies and epithets are weighted according to each officer's abilities and proficiencies.
- **Personality Generation:** Assign 5–12 personality traits, accounting for attribute compatibility and mutually exclusive characteristics.
- **Equipment and Spirits:** Recommend equipment and elemental Spirits based on each officer's strengths and specialization.
- **Historical Birthplaces:** Generate birthplaces from 76 in-game cities and their respective provinces.
- **Customizable Generation:** Lock individual settings, manually override selections, and reroll characters.
- **Character Export:** Save generated officers as TXT or JSON character sheets.
- **Fully Offline:** Runs directly in a modern web browser without installation, accounts, or an internet connection.

## Design Philosophy

The generator is primarily intended to create believable supporting officers who can naturally populate different factions throughout the Three Kingdoms era.

Not every officer is a legendary warrior or brilliant strategist. Some are capable administrators, ordinary commanders, provincial scholars, merchants, or ambitious wanderers.

The goal is to generate a diverse roster of characters with believable strengths, weaknesses, personalities, and histories while allowing players to customize the results.
