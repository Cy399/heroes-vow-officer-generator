Export

This app does not modify game files or click the game's interface. Enter generated settings manually.

Rules and caveats

Exactly one strategy per officer. Proficiency breadth and rank scale with overall and relevant attributes. A signature gear proficiency always rolls 80–89; Shield also receives a melee co-primary at 80–89. Attributes independently roll from 1–100; no combined budget. Talents are estimated from scores and are editable only within the game. Spirits use the five element categories and thematic suggestions, not a verified complete skill catalog. Affinity and narrative ties may not correspond to selectable mechanical relationships. Historical connections are fictional possibilities, not claims of documented kinship.
