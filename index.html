Generation controls

Strategies and epithets use in-game names. Equipment and Spirit skill selections are left to the player.

Enter a seed for a reproducible officer under the same settings.
Independent officers are designed to fit many scenarios and factions. Famous-person relationships are optional, not required.

Export

This app does not modify game files or click the game's interface. Enter generated settings manually.

Rules and caveats

Exactly one strategy per officer. Proficiency breadth and rank scale with overall and relevant attributes. A signature gear proficiency always rolls 80–89; Shield also receives a melee co-primary at 80–89. All six attributes have a floor of 10. Totals and individual caps follow the selected quality range. Talents are estimated from scores and are editable only within the game. Spirit skills are not generated. Affinity and narrative ties may not correspond to selectable mechanical relationships. Historical connections are fictional possibilities, not claims of documented kinship.
